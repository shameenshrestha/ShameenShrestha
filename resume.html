Final project for Neuro-Symbolic AI · Fall 2025 · NJIT

LLMs · Logic · Neuro-Symbolic AI

  • Implemented a Symbolic Working Memory (SWM) framework that augments LLMs with explicit fact and rule tracking.
  • Extended SWM to propositional and first-order logic, supporting variable binding, quantifiers, negation, and multi-step inference.

Adversarial Vulnerability in Low-ID vs High-ID Regions

Final project for Dimensionality and Scalability in AI · Spring 2025 · NJIT

CNNs · Adversarial ML · Robustness Analysis
