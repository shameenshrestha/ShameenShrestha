Explainable AI & Interpretability Researcher

AI that can be understood, not just used.

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at NJIT, researching explainability, interpretability, and attribution in AI. Modern AI systems are remarkably capable, yet the processes behind their predictions remain largely hidden. My research develops methods to identify the evidence that drives those predictions and understand how models represent and use it internally. I also study how to evaluate explanations, ensuring they faithfully reflect what a model computes rather than what we expect to see.
