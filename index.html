Shameen Shrestha

I study how large language models reason, and how they fail. My work sits at the meeting point of LLM reasoning, adversarial robustness, and neuro-symbolic AI.

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology. My current projects give LLMs an explicit symbolic working memory for multi-step logical inference, stress-test summarization models with adversarial perturbations, and connect the local geometry of data to adversarial vulnerability.

At NJIT I’m a teaching assistant for courses in generative AI, machine learning, cybersecurity, and databases.

Before my PhD I spent several years as a software engineer at LIS Nepal, building ETL pipelines in Snowflake and dashboards in Power BI and Tableau. I’ve also volunteered with Women in Big Data Nepal, running workshops and mentorship programs for students.

Projects

Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning with Symbolic Working Memory

LLMs · Logic · Neuro-Symbolic AI

Augments LLMs with explicit fact and rule tracking, extended to propositional and first-order logic. Evaluated on ProofWriter and PrOntoQA, with more stable reasoning under rule shuffling and noisy premises.

Adversarial Attacks on LLM Summarization

LLMs · Hugging Face · NLP Robustness

Character-, word-, and sentence-level attacks on T5, BART, PEGASUS, and DistilBART over CNN/DailyMail, measured by attack success rate, ROUGE drop, and SBERT similarity, with cross-model transfer analysis.

Adversarial Vulnerability in Low-ID vs High-ID Regions

CNNs · Adversarial ML · Robustness

FGSM, PGD, and DeepFool on standard and adversarially trained CNNs show that high-LID regions have sharper loss surfaces and higher attack success than low-LID regions.

Real-Time Sign Language Detection

Deep Learning · Computer Vision

A PyTorch CNN with live OpenCV video processing, running end-to-end from camera input to gesture classification in real time.
